Written by :

Derrick Henry and Jonathan Taylor have the highest projected rushing yards total for the upcoming regular season, and those over/unders are 650 yards shy of 2K.

SportsBetting.ag released more than 60 running back props for individual statistical totals. Rushing yards and rushing TDs are available for nearly every RB while a select few also have rushing + receiving yards and TDs props.
