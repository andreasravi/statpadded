Reading it

cleared the receiving-yards line (OVER) fell short (UNDER) INJ played fewer than 14 games

From 2023 on, each row is graded three ways independently: yds, rec, and TD. The 2022 article published a receiving-yards line only, so its rec and TD cells show the actual total with no line to grade against. The colored edge on the name always reflects the yards result. The Δ columns are actual minus line — positive means the receiver beat the number.

Tap any column header to re-sort. Default sort is by yards Δ, biggest overperformance first.

Caveats

Prop lines are transcribed from the articles' published grids (juice/vig not shown, so a push at the exact half-point never happens — every line ends in .5). "INJ" seasons mostly missed the number on availability, not on per-game form — worth separating before drawing talent conclusions. Actuals are regular season only; playoff production is excluded, matching how the lines were framed.
