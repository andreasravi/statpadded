2026 vs the last two years

Every August the claim comes up that Vegas has quietly lowered season-long yardage lines to correct the historical over-bias. This is the distribution of the 20 highest lines at each position, 2024 → 2025 → 2026. They haven't moved. Median receiving-yards line: 1,050 → 1,063 → 1,050. Median rushing-yards line: 900 → 938 → 935.
